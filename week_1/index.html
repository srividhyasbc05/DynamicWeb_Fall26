Aloo Curry

  • Indian
  • Vegetarian
  • 4 russet potatoes, peeled and sliced in 1-inch chunks
  • ½ teaspoon Fennel Seeds
  • ½ teaspoon Cumin Seeds
  • 2 tablespoons ghee, unsalted butter, or coconut oil
  • 1 medium yellow onion, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 large tomato, diced
  • 1-2 tablespoons Curry Powder
  • 1-2 tablespoons Chili Powder
  • 1-2 Bay Leaves
  • Salt to taste
  • Cilantro for garnish

Instructions

Making the Masala

If you prefer to prepare the potatoes in advance, you can parboil them until they are tender and set aside

  1. Heat ghee or butter in a large skillet on medium heat
  2. Add the spices on a low to medium flame. Fry cumin and fennel seeds for a few minutes.
  3. Add onions, garlic, and a pinch of salt once the cumin seeds start to splutter. Cook until the onions are golden.
  4. Add ginger and garlic and saute until a nice aroma comes from the mixture. Add the curry and chili powder as well for flavor. Stir until well combined.
  5. Add bay leaf, cover the pan, and simmer for 5-10 minutes.
  6. Add the diced tomato and cook until the tomato is soft and mushy.
  7. Add any spices, including chili powder or garam masala.

Adding the Potatoes

  1. Add the chopped potatoes to the masala and stir until they are well coated. Stir until everything has a smooth consistency.
  2. Add water to the pan and cover it with a lid. Simmer for 15-20 minutes or until the potatoes are tender (if it can be pierced with a fork and come out smoothly).
